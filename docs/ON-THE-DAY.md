# On the day

## The card set-up moment (15 minutes)

Give everyone a card at the door, then run one short moment where the whole room sets them up together. Early in the day works best: people swap all day after that.

**Before it starts**
- Open your big-screen page on the TV or projector: `/screen` on your live site (the link is in `my-links.md`). Press **F** for full screen. It shows a big QR and the 4 steps.
- Tap a spare card on your own phone to check the venue wi-fi or phone signal is working. Every tap loads a page.

**What to say (roughly)**
1. "Grab your card. Scan the code on the screen with your phone camera."
2. "Pop in your name and your socials. Only add what you'd hand a stranger."
3. "Now hold your card flat to the top of the back of your phone, near the camera. Tap the pop-up. Hit save."
4. "That's your pass. Now find three people near you and tap your card on their phone."

People who scan the QR type their details first, so when they tap their card it's all filled in: one tap of save. People who tap their card straight away can fill it in there. Both work.

## Tapping tips (put these on a slide, or say them)

- **iPhone:** the card goes at the **top of the back**, near the camera. A pop-up appears at the top of the screen: tap it. iPhone XS and newer do this with no app. Older iPhones (7, 8, X): open Control Centre and use the NFC Tag Reader button.
- **Android:** the **middle of the back**. If nothing happens, NFC is off: Settings, search "NFC", turn it on.
- **Thick case or a wallet on the back?** Take it off for a second.
- **Phone won't tap at all?** The owner opens their pass, hits **show my QR** at the bottom, and the other person scans it with their camera.
- **NFC Tools open on your phone?** Close it. It grabs the tap.

## Helpers

Have one or two people who know the drill walk the room during the set-up moment. The most common fixes: wrong spot on the phone, a thick case, and holding the card too briefly.

## If something goes wrong

| What people see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens when they tap | wrong spot, a case, or NFC off (Android). Try the top edge (iPhone) or middle (Android), case off |
| "not one of our cards" | that card was written from a test list or the wrong address. Give them a spare, and write that card again later |
| "someone just set this card up" | two people tapped a blank card at the same moment (or a mix-up at the door). Give the second person a spare |
| The wrong person set up a card | **Reset** it on the admin page, then they tap it again |
| "couldn't load" | signal. Move closer to the wi-fi, or try again in a sec |

## After the event

- Cards keep working for as long as your site is live. People can keep using them at the next event.
- Someone asks for their details to be removed: **Reset** their card on the admin page.
- Leftover blank cards: keep them for next time. They're numbered, so the next batch carries on from your highest number.
- If you turned on email edit links, the admin page shows those emails. People gave them only for edit links, so don't add them to a mailing list.
